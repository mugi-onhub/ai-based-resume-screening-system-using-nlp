Data Scientist - Job Description

We are looking for a Data Scientist with strong Python skills and experience
in machine learning and NLP. The ideal candidate has hands-on experience with
scikit-learn, TensorFlow or PyTorch, and has deployed models using Docker or
cloud platforms such as AWS or Azure. Experience with SQL, data visualization
(Power BI or Tableau), and version control (Git) is required. Familiarity with
Streamlit or Flask for building demo applications is a plus. Strong communication
skills and the ability to explain model results to non-technical stakeholders
are essential.

Responsibilities:
- Build and evaluate machine learning models for business problems.
- Clean and analyze large datasets using Python and SQL.
- Communicate findings through dashboards and reports.
- Collaborate with engineering to deploy models to production.

Requirements:
- 2+ years of experience with Python, scikit-learn, and SQL.
- Experience with at least one deep learning framework (TensorFlow or PyTorch).
- Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP).
- Experience with Git and collaborative development.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, or a related field.
